# Statistics 

- Mean: The average; sum of all values divided by the number of values. Good for symmetrical data, but outliers can heavily skew it (e.g., average house price can be misleading).
- Median: The middle number when data is sorted from lowest to highest; splits data in half (50% above, 50% below).  Better for skewed data or data with outliers because it represents the true midpoint (e.g., typical household income).
- Mode: The value that appears most often in the dataset. Best for non-numerical (categorical) data or to find the most popular item (e.g., most common hair color)
- Range: - how spread data is.

# Logic 

Law of Identity
    A thing is identical to itself (A = A).
    - **Meaning**: Everything has a distinct identity and properties that make it what it is.
    - **Example**: A cat is a cat.
    
Law of Non-Contradiction
    A statement and its negation cannot both be true at the same time (not (p and not-p)).
    - **Meaning**: A proposition cannot be both true and false simultaneously.
    - **Example**: It cannot be raining and not raining at the exact same moment.
    
Law of the Excluded Middle
    A proposition is either true or false; there's no third option (p or not-p).
    - **Meaning**: A statement must be true or false, with no in-between possibility.
    - **Example**: The statement "This key opens the door" is either true or false, not partially true.
